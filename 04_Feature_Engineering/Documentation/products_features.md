**1. product\_volume\_cm3**



Description:

Represents the physical volume of a product in cubic centimeters.



Business Purpose:

Helps analyze shipping requirements, warehouse storage utilization, packaging efficiency, and the impact of product size on logistics.



Formula:

product\_length\_cm × product\_height\_cm × product\_width\_cm



Source Column(s):

product\_length\_cm

product\_height\_cm

product\_width\_cm



Data Type:

float64



Expected Values:

Positive numeric values



Missing Values:

Same as source dimensions



Example:

Length = 20 cm

Width = 10 cm

Height = 5 cm



Volume = 1000 cm³
